# Evidence, gaps and the calibration loop

Use this to decide how hard to lean on any rule in the skill.

## Firmly supported

- The opening must confirm what the title and thumbnail promised (YouTube's own guidance).
- Curiosity comes from a specific gap the viewer can feel and peaks when they know something but not everything (small controlled studies).
- Viewers holding a wrong belief tune out correct explanations, so name and defeat the misconception first (a creator's own research).
- Chaining beats with "but" and "therefore", not "and then", is the best-documented structural engine.
- Plain words, one idea per sentence and a read-aloud test are consistent across voice-over guidance.
- Use only verified facts, label estimates and artist's concepts, and keep the promise.

## Hypotheses to test, not rules

- Hook timings and the 45 to 75 second window; 30-second retention thresholds (50, 60 and 65 percent conflict across vendors).
- Re-hook cadence and a fixed number of seconds between pattern interrupts. No study tests them.
- The "MrBeast changes something every 60 seconds" line is not in the reported memo and is misattributed.
- Word-per-minute figures and chapter lengths (small samples, captions).
- Everything in the Hinglish register layer beyond the user's own scripts: the "aap plus imperatives" pattern comes from three Hindi caption files.
- Deflation jokes, named actors and scale ladders: observed in a handful of videos with no retention data.
- Whether the Zeigarnik effect supports open loops: contested. Justify loops by information-gap instead.

## Gaps

- No independent platform-wide hook or retention data.
- Almost no Hindi or Hinglish craft evidence outside the reference transcripts: register, which English words to keep, speed, retention norms, title performance.
- No script-level analysis of PBS Space Time, Cool Worlds, Scott Manley, Fraser Cain, Anton Petrov, Kyle Hill, Dr. Becky or Wendover.
- ElevenLabs and Google behaviour on Hinglish input is unverified beyond the user's own tests.
- Official YouTube policy text was not retrieved; policy statements rest on news reports.
- NASA, ESA and other agency reuse terms were not verified.

## Calibration loop (run it)

1. Time a real render of 60 seconds of the actual script, count the words, and update the default speed in `SKILL.md`.
2. After publishing, read the retention graph at 48 hours and 7 days. Log the hook type and the 30-second figure.
3. A sharp early drop means rewrite the hook; a slow mid-video slide means add loops and re-hooks.
4. Replace a default here only with the channel's own result.
5. Add new reference transcripts when the user supplies them and extend `observed-patterns.md`.
